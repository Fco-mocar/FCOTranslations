Services

Localization

Full localization services for games, apps, and interactive media. I adapt dialogue, UI, in-game systems, marketing assets, and culture-specific references for your target audience.

  • Translation + cultural adaptation
  • Terminology management & glossary creation
  • UI/UX strings, tooltips, system messages
  • Quality assurance and linguistic testing

Transcription

Accurate transcription for interviews, podcasts, videos and live recordings, ready for captioning or translation workflows.

  • Time-coded transcripts (SRT, VTT)
  • Speaker identification & formatting
  • Turnaround options for tight deadlines
  • Confidentiality and secure file handling

Subtitling

Subtitle creation that preserves tone, timing, and readability across all screen sizes. I deliver file formats ready for streaming platforms and broadcast.

  • Translation + subtitle editing
  • Timecoding and frame-accurate sync
  • Style guide compliance (e.g., Netflix, YouTube)
  • Closed captions & SDH (subtitles for the deaf and hard of hearing)

Dubbing, Translation & Adaptation

Script adaptation for dubbing and voice-over, prioritizing lip sync, rhythm, and character voice while keeping the original meaning.

  • Lip-sync adaptation and phrasing
  • Character voice and tone preservation
  • Collaboration with directors and voice talent
  • On-set localization support
