Localization

Full localization services for games, apps, and interactive content. I adapt dialogue, interface text, systems, and cultural references for your target audience.

  • Translation + cultural adaptation
  • Terminology and glossary management
  • UI/UX strings, tooltips, system messages
  • Quality checks and linguistic testing

Transcription

Accurate transcriptions for interviews, podcasts, videos, and live recordings, ready for subtitling or translation workflows.

  • Time-stamped transcripts (SRT, VTT)
  • Speaker identification and formatting
  • Fast-turnaround options for tight deadlines
  • Confidential handling and secure file delivery

Subtitling

Subtitles that preserve tone, timing, and readability on any screen. Delivered in streaming-ready formats for global distribution.

  • Translation + subtitle editing
  • Precise timecoding and sync
  • Style guide compliance (e.g., Netflix, YouTube)
  • Closed captions for accessibility

Dubbing & Adaptation

Script adaptation for dubbing and voice-over, prioritizing lip sync, pacing, and character voice without losing original meaning.

  • Lip-sync and phrasing adaptation
  • Voice and tone preservation
  • Collaboration with directors and talent
  • On-set localization support
